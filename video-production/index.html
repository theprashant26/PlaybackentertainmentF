Introduction

At Playback Media & Events Hub, filmmaking and video production are central to our creative identity.

Our work spans television productions, corporate films, advertising films, documentaries, promotional content, interviews, testimonials, training videos, conference recordings, and digital media.

From creative planning and filming to editing, motion graphics, sound processing, and final delivery, our services are designed to create videos that communicate clearly and professionally.

We develop each production according to the message, audience, distribution platform, timeline, and approved creative requirements.

FAQs

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? Call or WhatsApp us and we will be happy to help.

How long does a corporate film take to produce?

A typical corporate film takes two to four weeks from script approval to delivery, depending on locations, interviews and graphics.

Do you write scripts?

Yes. We develop concepts and scripts with you, or work from your approved script.

Can you deliver versions for social media?

Yes. We deliver landscape, square and vertical versions, short cut-downs and subtitled versions as required.

Do you have television production experience?

Yes. Our leadership has directed more than 500 television episodes, including the quiz show Aao Baniye Gursikh Pyara.
