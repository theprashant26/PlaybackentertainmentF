FAQs

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? Call or WhatsApp us and we will be happy to help.

Do you travel outside Delhi NCR for shoots?

Yes. We are based in Ghaziabad and work across Delhi NCR and North India, and we can plan assignments in other locations depending on the scope.

How quickly will we receive our photographs?

Timelines depend on the volume and editing involved. We agree delivery dates during planning, and urgent selections for social media or press can be prioritised.

Can you photograph at our office or factory?

Yes. We bring portable lighting and backdrops for on-location corporate portraits, team photographs and workplace or industrial photography.

Do you provide edited and retouched images?

Every assignment includes professional selection and editing. Detailed retouching for portraits, products or advertising can be included as needed.
