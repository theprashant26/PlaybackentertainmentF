Choosing LED Displays for Conferences

By Playback Media & Events Hub · 14 September 2026 · 2 min read

LED walls have become the centrepiece of modern conference stages. Bright, seamless and flexible, they display presentations, videos and branding in one large canvas. Choosing the right configuration makes a significant difference to how your event looks.

Size for the room

The display should be large enough for delegates at the back to read slides comfortably. Consider the hall depth, ceiling height and seating layout before deciding dimensions.

Pixel pitch

Pixel pitch is the distance between LED pixels. A smaller pitch provides a sharper image at closer viewing distances. For most conference halls, the right pitch balances clarity with budget.

Brightness and lighting

LED walls are bright enough for well-lit venues, but brightness should be adjusted for the room and for camera recording so faces and slides look natural on video.

Content format

LED walls are often wider than standard 16:9 presentations. Prepare branding and backgrounds for the full wall, and plan how speaker slides will sit within the layout.

Technical support

A trained operator should manage switching between presentations, videos and camera feeds. Rehearsals help catch format issues before the audience arrives.
