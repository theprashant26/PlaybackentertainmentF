Our story

From the lens to the full picture.

Playback Media & Events Hub is a Delhi NCR-based creative production, digital marketing, and event management company.

Our professional journey began with photography, videography, television production, and visual storytelling.

Through years of practical production experience, we developed our expertise in corporate films, advertising videos, television programs, commercial photography, portfolios, interviews, and professional post-production.

In 2018, we expanded into digital marketing, helping businesses strengthen their presence through websites, search optimization, social media, advertising, and creative communication.

Our experience working at corporate events and medical conferences gave us valuable insight into event production, audiovisual systems, conference arrangements, and technical execution. Building on this foundation, we expanded into event and conference management.

Today, Playback provides coordinated solutions across photography, video production, digital marketing, and event management for businesses, brands, healthcare professionals, institutions, and organizations.
