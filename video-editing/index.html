Videography & Film Production

Video Editing & Post-Production

Turning footage into polished, purposeful films.

Send Us Your Footage View Our Work
Overview

Our editing services transform recorded footage into polished videos through storytelling, sound refinement, color correction, graphics, captions, and final processing.

We edit our own productions and footage supplied by clients, from conference recordings and highlight films to motion graphic explainers and multi-language versions.

What’s included

  • Story-led editing
  • Colour correction & grading
  • Audio cleanup & mixing
  • Subtitles & captions
  • Motion graphics & titles
  • Versions for every platform
  • Live multi-camera editing at events

FAQs

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? Call or WhatsApp us and we will be happy to help.

Can you edit footage we have already shot?

Yes. Share your footage and requirements, and we will advise on what is possible and the timeline.

How many revisions are included?

We agree the number of review rounds in the proposal; most projects include two rounds of changes.

Can you add subtitles in Hindi and English?

Yes. We create subtitles and captions in English, Hindi and other languages on request.
