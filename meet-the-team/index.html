The people behind the creative work

Meet Our Team

Creative Minds. Technical Expertise. One Shared Vision.

Behind every photograph, film, digital campaign, and professionally executed event is a team of individuals combining creativity, experience, technology, and dedication.

Explore Careers Join Freelancer Network

At Playback Media & Events Hub, our strength lies in the people who bring ideas to life.

Our professional network spans photography, cinematography, filmmaking, video editing, digital marketing, design, audiovisual production, and event coordination. Together, we work to develop meaningful content, effective brand communication, and memorable professional experiences.

Leadership

Leading the vision.

Raushan S Kumar

Founder & Director

Raushan S Kumar leads Playback Media & Events Hub with extensive experience in photography, videography, television production, filmmaking, and business development.

His professional journey began in media production, developing practical expertise across corporate films, commercial shoots, television assignments, professional photography, and visual storytelling.

As the business evolved, he expanded its services into digital marketing and subsequently into event management, particularly medical conferences, corporate events, and production services.

His vision is to build Playback into an integrated creative, digital, and event production company recognized for professional execution, innovation, and strong client relationships.

Neha Singh

Director

Neha Singh contributes to the leadership and creative development of Playback Media & Events Hub.

With a background in digital marketing and creative communication, she brings an understanding of branding, digital engagement, and organizational development.

Her involvement supports the company's approach to creative thinking, communication, and long-term business growth.
