Photography

Event & Conference Photography

Every speaker, delegate and important moment, professionally documented.

Book Event Photography View Our Work
Overview

From keynote speakers and delegates to exhibitions, awards, and networking, our event photography documents important professional experiences.

Our photographers understand conference programmes, multi-hall sessions and stage lighting, so we are in the right place for every key moment without disturbing the proceedings.

What’s included

  • Keynote, speaker & panel photography
  • Delegate, networking & candid coverage
  • Award & felicitation moments
  • Exhibition & sponsor stall coverage
  • Multi-hall coverage with multiple photographers
  • Same-day selections for social media
Why it works

How we help.

Speakers & sessions

Clear photographs of every speaker and session, including LED stages and low light.

Awards & ceremonies

Every presentation captured, with group photographs organised quickly.

FAQs

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? Call or WhatsApp us and we will be happy to help.

Can you cover multiple halls at a conference?

Yes. We assign photographers to each hall and coordinate coverage of keynotes, workshops and exhibitions.

Can we get photographs during the event?

Yes. We can deliver selected edited images during or at the end of each day for social media and press.

Do you also provide event videography?

Yes. Our video team can provide session recordings, interviews and highlight films alongside photography.
